England Tour of India T20I 2021 Team India is scheduled to play 3 T20Is against England from 7 July(2022) – 10 July (2022). Rohit Sharma and Jos Buttler, the two dynamic openers, will be leading their respective teams in the series which serves as a preparation to fine-tune their best squad for the T20 World Cup in Australia. The last time India and England played against each other in T20I was in a 5-match T20I bilateral series from March 12 (2021) – March(2021) at Narendra Modi Stadium, Ahmedabad.
