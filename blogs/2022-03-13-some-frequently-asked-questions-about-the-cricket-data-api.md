FAQ: Frequently Asked Questions about the Cricket Live Scores API Will buying different pricing tiered plans have different features? The difference between Lifetime Free and Paid plans is the number of requests you are allowed to make to fetch Match, Score, Series, etc data. You will have access to all of the Cricket Data API’s features from the Lifetime Free plan.
